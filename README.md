# MorseMentor

**Learn, practise and decode Morse code.** Developed by **Shuvan CoderHub**.

## ⬇️ Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 14 or later) | `MorseMentor-Mac.dmg` |
| Windows PC (Intel/AMD, Windows 10 or 11) | `MorseMentor-Windows-x64.zip` |
| Windows on ARM (e.g. Snapdragon laptops) | `MorseMentor-Windows-ARM64.zip` |

**Mac:** open the DMG and drag MorseMentor into Applications. The first time, macOS asks before
opening it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

**Windows:** unzip and double-click `MorseMentor.exe`. Nothing needs installing. If Windows shows
"Windows protected your PC", click **More info › Run anyway**.

## What it does

- **Send text as Morse:** adjustable character speed, Farnsworth text speed, pitch, rise/fall and
  tone; 949 practice texts, your own text files, or random groups; the text is highlighted as it
  plays; save the sound as a WAV file.
- **Teach:** learn the characters in Koch, Howard, Alpha/Num or your own order. New characters are
  added as you master them, with a mastery rating and an error chart.
- **Listening Mode:** decodes Morse live from the microphone or from audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A…),
  at any pitch from 300 to 3000 Hz, and ignores speech, music and noise. Shows the dots and dashes
  and the decoded text, with live frequency, signal and speed.

Based on the NZART Morse Teaching Program by ZL1AN (SK) and ZL1AF, which supplied the practice
texts and teaching orders.
